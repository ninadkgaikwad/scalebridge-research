SIM3 HVAC DATA CONTRACT
-----------------------
Phase-B sequences: Tz, direct-air-inlet Ts, m_dot, sensible heating, sensible cooling.
Signed QAC target: Q_heating - Q_cooling.
Phase-C predictor: 1000*1.005*m_dot*(Ts-Tz).

For all-to-one, simultaneous source-zone heating/cooling is permitted. Effective mode
uses signed NET QAC together with sign(Ts-Tz), not a one-component-only assumption.

MODE VALUES
-----------
Ts_cool / Ts_heat:
    mass-flow-weighted median of training rows classified in that effective mode.

m_dot_nominal:
    median active flow for that mode.

m_dot_max:
    maximum finite active training flow for that mode.

Sim3 user choice:
    cooling: nominal|max
    heating: nominal|max

The calibration also stores P01/P05/P10/P25/P50/P75/P90/P95/P99 flow/Tz summaries so
nominal/max choices remain auditable.

DEADBANDS
---------
Data-derived values are always computed and saved. Overrides are allowed and both
`*_data_C` and `*_used_C` plus source=data|override are retained in provenance.

SETPOINTS
---------
Default calibration uses data quantiles 10/50/90 percent as low/medium/high. They are
stored in the calibration artifact; final paper runs can freeze a deliberate policy
after inspecting the real calibration report.

IMPORTANT DATA-READINESS RULE
-----------------------------
Calibration records unavailable modes as such. The real validator deliberately refuses
to invent a missing heating/cooling Ts or m_dot. If a zone has no effective samples for
one mode in TRAIN, inspect the calibration output and define an explicit scientifically
justified policy before final Sim3 paper runs.

SIM3 METRICS FAIRNESS
---------------------
Because Sim3 deliberately replaces historical QAC with thermostat-generated QAC,
historical test Tz is saved only as a reference trace. It is not used as a prediction
accuracy target. Primary Sim3 metrics are setpoint RMSE/MAE, within-deadband fraction,
degree-hours outside deadband, PHVAC energy/peak, m_dot use, and HVAC/mode switching.
