LOCKED ACTUATOR-PARAMETER HIERARCHY
-----------------------------------
For each zone/mode:
1. explicit per-mode override, if deliberately supplied;
2. observed same-mode TRAIN values: mass-flow-weighted median Ts, median nominal mdot, maximum mdot;
3. if the mode is absent, same-zone observed opposite-mode fallback only.

The missing-mode fallback uses:
- the observed opposite-mode mass-flow-weighted active |Ts-Tz|;
- that same zone's medium setpoint as the reflection center;
- a physical sign guard (heating Ts > high setpoint; cooling Ts < low setpoint);
- the same zone's observed opposite-mode nominal and max mdot.

It does NOT use equipment definitions and does NOT search other weather/runs.

CONTROLLER CAPABILITY
---------------------
Every zone may request and physically apply heating or cooling regardless of TRAIN mode support. TRAIN support is provenance/confidence only. Unobserved-mode Phase-C QAC evaluation is allowed and flagged extrapolative/OOD. No action is suppressed solely because the requested mode was absent from TRAIN.

MASS FLOW + DEADBANDS
---------------------
Cooling and heating active-flow choices are independently `nominal | max`. Deadbands are data-derived by default and overrideable; both data and used values are saved.

SETPOINTS
---------
Low/high = strict-TRAIN P10/P90. Medium = P50 only when separated by >=0.5 C from both; otherwise midpoint(low,high). This repairs Dining's P10≈P50 collapse.

SIM3
----
Historical test QAC is never replayed. Sim3 records requested/delivered mode, observed-in-TRAIN support, actuator parameter source, extrapolative QAC flag, Ts, mdot, QAC and PHVAC.
