PATCH 01 FINAL HEAT CONTRACT

Authoritative channel classification:
  Convective = QZIC + QSol1 + QAC
  Radiative  = QZIR + QSol2

1R1C:
  Qeff = Qconv + eta_rad * Qrad
  eta_rad modes: full (default=1), zero (ablation=0), fixed, learnable.
  eta_rad is an effective radiative participation factor because 1R1C has no
  explicit mass/surface state. Convective channels keep unit gain.

2R2C:
  Qconv always enters air.
  Qrad_mass = eta_rad * Qrad
  Qrad_air  = (1-eta_rad) * Qrad
  eta_rad modes: mass_only (default=1), air_only (0), fixed, learnable.
  Radiative heat is conserved across air+mass.

There is no separate QAC gain and no independent per-channel gain in the main model.
