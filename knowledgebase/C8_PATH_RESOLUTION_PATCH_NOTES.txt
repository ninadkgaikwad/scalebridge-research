C8 path-resolution patch v2

Fixes discovery when a C6 training manifest embeds the C4 manifest under
source_dataset_manifest_payload but does not contain a separate
source_dataset_manifest path.

Resolution order:
1. source_dataset_manifest
2. source_dataset_manifest_path
3. embedded manifest_path/model_dataset_manifest
4. embedded output_root/model_dataset_manifest.json
5. parent of embedded outputs.train/model_dataset_manifest.json

The resolved path must exist and be a file.
