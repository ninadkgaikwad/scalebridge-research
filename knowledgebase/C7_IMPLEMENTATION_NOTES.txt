ScaleBridge Stage C7 Heat-Input Regression Evaluation

C7 loads persisted C6 artifacts and evaluates the original C4 train,
validation, and test Parquets without refitting. It writes split metrics,
full prediction/residual Parquets, previews, generalization summaries, and
provenance manifests. The independent validator reloads the model and source
splits, recomputes predictions and RMSE/MAE, and verifies saved predictions.
