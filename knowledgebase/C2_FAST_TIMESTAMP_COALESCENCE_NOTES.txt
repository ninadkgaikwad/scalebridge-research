ScaleBridge Fast C2 Timestamp Coalescence Patch v6
===================================================

Reason
------
The v5 implementation iterated through every parsed timestamp group in Python.
For a one-year 5-minute dataset, this meant approximately 105,120 group
iterations per zone, even though only a small number of timestamps were
duplicated.

Optimization
------------
- Parse and normalize the complete timestamp column.
- Split rows into unique-timestamp and duplicate-timestamp subsets.
- Pass unique rows through vectorially without Python iteration.
- Iterate only the small duplicate subset.
- Preserve column-wise coalescence, densest-row preference, conflict auditing,
  metadata, and canonical ordering.

The output semantics are unchanged; only execution speed is improved.
