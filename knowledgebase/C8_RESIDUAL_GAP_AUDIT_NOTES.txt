Purpose
-------
Investigate the five remaining canonical timestamps and 57 missing component
values after the C2 duplicate-timestamp correction.

The audit produces:
- event-level residual missing records;
- timestamp-level summary;
- component/zone summary;
- source-family classification;
- ±2-step neighboring source values;
- evidence-based treatment recommendations;
- JSON manifest and plain-text report.

The script does not modify C2 or C8 data and does not fill any values.

C9 and C10
----------
C9 and C10 remain pending. Their exact project definitions were not present in
the available handoff material, so this package deliberately does not invent
their behavior. The residual-gap outputs are designed to serve as a validated
input to the next stage definitions.
