RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARY
-----------------------
E0-8 owns generic HPO study/trial orchestration, Optuna persistence, MLflow
provenance, TRAIN-only enforcement, deterministic study/trial identity,
resume compatibility, standardized study artifacts, Pareto evidence, and
frozen hyperparameter configuration.

E.1/E.2/E.3/E.4 own all real hyperparameter names/domains/conditional science,
trial fitting/scoring, representative TRAIN-subset science, pruning scientific
meaning, and any deterministic final Pareto selection.

E0-8 freezes h*. It does NOT fit the final production model.
E.x performs final fitting; E0-7 packages the fitted result.

IMPORTANT HARDENING
-------------------
1. resume=True requires persistent Optuna storage_url. It cannot silently
   create a new in-memory study.
2. A non-disabled pruner is rejected unless the E.x provider explicitly
   authorizes pruning.
3. RecoverableTrialError is the only generic method-declared failure allowed to
   continue as a FAILED trial. Infrastructure exceptions terminate the study.
4. Provider-generated trial artifact references are retained in trials.parquet
   and logged into the matching nested MLflow trial run when tracking is enabled.
5. Multi-objective E0-8 preserves Pareto results. One frozen h* exists only if
   the provider selects exactly one Pareto member.
6. Study fingerprint intentionally follows the ratified formula and therefore
   includes study identity, method/provider version, search-space snapshot,
   objective contract, HPO-data fingerprint, study seed, and E0-8 schema.

DATA-LEAKAGE CONTRACT
---------------------
The E0-2 Phase-D temporal contract remains authoritative. E0-8 v1 accepts only
literal outer source_partitions=("train",). A provider may create an inner
fit/score/CV/representative subset only within TRAIN, and the exact selection
payload is fingerprinted.
