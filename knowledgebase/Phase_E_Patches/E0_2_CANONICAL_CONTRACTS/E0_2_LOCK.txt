ScaleBridge Phase E.0 — E0-2 Canonical Data + Scientific Contracts LOCK
============================================================================

Status
------
E0-2 canonical contract behavior is locked by this file and the corresponding
unit-contract tests.

Scope
-----
E0-2 defines only the method-neutral Phase D -> Phase E boundary.

It does not implement family-specific model mathematics.  Detailed mathematical
TeX contracts remain mandatory before implementation of any specific ML, SciML,
Optimization, or Bayesian method family/method.

Locked decisions
----------------

1. Phase D final manifest is authoritative.
   - No folder-name parsing to reconstruct scientific identity.
   - No Phase E redefinition of Phase D lag/horizon or outer partitions.

2. Exact signal binding.
   - Resolve by base_signal + aggregate_zone_id + exact lag/horizon.
   - No substring/heuristic column resolution in generic Phase E.

3. Zone-specific availability.
   - Different zones may expose different disturbances/controls.
   - A structurally uncontrolled zone may have no QAC.
   - Absence is not silently converted to zero.

4. QAC / PHVAC separation.
   - qac = signed thermal HVAC heat [W], control input.
   - phvac = HVAC electrical power [W], auxiliary/downstream output.
   - PHVAC is forbidden as a thermal-model input.

5. Observed / latent state separation.
   - Phase D zone_temperature is observed state.
   - E0-2 creates no artificial latent-state target.
   - Hidden RC states are declared later by topology/method math contracts.

6. Spatial products.
   - IND product models exactly its independent zone.
   - DEP1 models all current zones jointly.
   - DEP2 keeps current-zone states/controls and the Phase-D-selected compatible
     all-to-one disturbance source.
   - E0-2 does not create physical RC coupling edges.

7. Temporal ownership and tuning leakage.
   - Phase D owns outer partitions.
   - Representative HPO source rows are Phase D TRAIN only.
   - Inner fit/validation may be created inside that train-only HPO subset.
   - Phase D TEST may never be used for hyperparameter tuning.

8. Provenance.
   - Carry manifest provenance directly.
   - Preserve unknown provenance keys.
   - Do not infer missing identity from paths.
   - Hash canonical manifest content for exact source identity.

9. Controlled PINODE/EPSR paper implementation.
   - Scientific reference/oracle only.
   - Do not copy RestaurantFastFood/Buffalo/Dining/Kitchen constants, fixed
     forcing dimensions, string-substring resolution, or duplicated manual RC
     equations into generic Phase E.

10. E0-2 remains backend and method neutral.
    - RC physics begins E0-3.
    - arbitrary-zone coupling/latent initialization begins E0-4.
    - discretization begins E0-5.
    - backend parity begins E0-6.
    - runtime/PHVAC bundle composition begins E0-7.
    - common Optuna/MLflow lifecycle begins E0-8.
