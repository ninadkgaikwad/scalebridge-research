ScaleBridge Phase E0 - E0-8 Mathematics v1 Revision Notes
=========================================================
Date: 2026-08-31

This package installs the first ratified E0-8 mathematical/software-science
authority for the Generic Hyperparameter Optimization, Study Tracking, and
Frozen-Configuration Framework.

Key decisions:
- E0-8 is method-neutral.
- E.1/E.2/E.3/E.4 define all method-specific hyperparameters and objectives.
- Hyperparameters are distinct from fitted model parameters/weights/posteriors.
- Phase-D TRAIN only may influence HPO selection.
- Any HPO internal fit/score partition is derived from TRAIN only.
- Representative-subset science is method-owned, but E0-8 fingerprints/tracks it.
- Optuna is the optimization authority; MLflow is tracking/provenance authority.
- Single-objective and multi-objective/Pareto studies are supported generically.
- E0-8 does not invent multi-objective scalarization.
- COMPLETE, PRUNED, FAILED remain distinct.
- Study resume requires exact immutable fingerprint compatibility.
- E0-8 freezes h*; E.x performs final production-model fitting.
- Final fitted model is subsequently packaged through E0-7.
- E0-10 remains the comprehensive real testing campaign.
