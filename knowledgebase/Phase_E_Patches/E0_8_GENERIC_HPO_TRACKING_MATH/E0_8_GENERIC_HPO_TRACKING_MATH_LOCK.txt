------------------------------

tracking/provenance, TRAIN-only leakage enforcement, reproducible study/trial
identity, compatible resume, trial terminal-state semantics, Pareto bookkeeping,

posterior representations Pi_Theta.

ranking, Pareto construction, or final HPO selection.
